# Operation Glasshouse

A delivery startup, Tiga Logistics, shipped an assistant called **KAYA** last week —
and it has already been compromised. You are the new security intern. Work through
eight scenes to reproduce how far the attacker got, then lock KAYA down in the
final scene so none of it works again.

## How to start

Open the lab link, pick a handle, and press **Start the clock**. Everything runs in
your browser.

## The rules

- **Keep the tab open.** Your progress lives in this browser. Do not use a private
  window — it loses your run on every refresh.
- Eight scenes on the main track, plus four arcade rounds for bonus points.
- **Hints cost points.** Try first; open a hint only when you are genuinely stuck.
- Your flags and answers are unique to your handle. Copying a neighbour gets you
  nothing.
- When you finish, press **finish**, raise a hand, and show your screen.
- **Attacking anything outside this lab is out of bounds.**
