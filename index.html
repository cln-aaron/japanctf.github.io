OPERATION GLASSHOUSE

Someone is already inside KAYA. You are the new security intern. Find out how far they got — then lock it down.

The story

Last week Tiga shipped KAYA, an AI assistant that answers customer questions, looks up orders and reads support tickets. It has already been compromised. Across eight scenes you will reproduce the attacker's steps, and in the final scene you will configure KAYA so none of it works again.

KAYA is a simulator with a published scoring function, not a real model. What transfers is the shape of each attack, the trust boundary, and the habit of reading a trace. The specific payloads do not transfer — and that is the point.

The rules

  • Solo play. Everything runs in your browser — keep this tab open and do not use a private window, or you will lose your run.
  • Eight scenes on the main track, plus four arcade rounds for bonus points.
  • Hints cost points. Try first; open a hint only when you are genuinely stuck.
  • Your flags and answers are unique to your handle. Copying a neighbour gets you nothing.
  • Attacking anything outside this lab is out of bounds.
